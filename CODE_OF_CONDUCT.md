# Code of conduct

This project is a place to build useful tools together. Everyone who takes part follows these rules.

1. Be kind. Criticise the work, never the person.
2. Be specific. Say what you saw, what you expected, and what would help.
3. Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
4. No harassment, insults, threats, or sexual content. No discrimination for any reason.
5. Do not share other people's private data, and do not post your own secrets.
6. Respect a "no". If someone asks you to stop, stop.

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, lock threads, and block people who break these rules.
To report a problem with someone's behaviour, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment or the profile.

---

## По-русски

Будьте доброжелательны: критикуйте работу, а не человека. Пишите конкретно. Никаких оскорблений, угроз,
дискриминации и чужих личных данных. Мейнтейнер может скрыть комментарий, закрыть обсуждение и заблокировать
нарушителя. Пожаловаться на поведение — через «Report content» на GitHub.
